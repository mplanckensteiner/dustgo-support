# DustGo Support

DustGo pauses your Mac's keyboard for a short time so you can clean it without typing into other apps.

## Getting started

1. Open DustGo from the menu bar (the keyboard icon).
2. The first time, DustGo asks for Accessibility access. Click **Open System Settings** and turn on DustGo under Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
3. Choose a duration, then click **Start Cleaning**.
4. Clean your keyboard. Keys you press are ignored.

## Ending a cleaning session

A session ends in any of these ways:

- Click **Finish Cleaning**. This option isn't available while the trackpad is paused.
- Hold **Esc** for 3 seconds.
- Let the timer run out. Sessions last at most 5 minutes, plus any time you add with the **+30 Seconds** button.

Sessions also end automatically when your Mac goes to sleep, the screen locks, or you switch users.

## Frequently asked questions

**DustGo says it needs Accessibility access, but I already turned it on.**
In System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility, select DustGo, remove it with the − button, then add it again with +. This often happens after an update.

**Some keys still work during cleaning.**
macOS doesn't let apps block Touch ID, the power button, or some keys handled by your Mac's firmware. Brightness, volume, and media keys may also behave differently depending on your keyboard and settings.

**The trackpad still does some things while paused.**
Some system gestures, like Mission Control and swiping between Spaces, are handled by macOS and may still respond. Connected mice are paused too, because macOS doesn't say which device an event came from.

**How do I clean my screen?**
Choose **Clean Screen** from the menu bar or the main window. Your displays turn a solid color so dust and smudges are easy to see. Press Esc to finish. You can choose the color in Settings.

**Does DustGo collect my data?**
No. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).

## Requirements

macOS 26 or later.
